What I learned — lives here

—

Smallest policy that fits. A wallet you find annoying is a wallet you empty into a worse one.

—

Two makers, not two phones. MuSig2 hides the second signer (one 64-byte sig); 2-of-3 advertises it — and that advertisement is the coercion deterrent.

—

Phone does the matching. Peers send public filters; the phone checks locally. Never asks a server “which addresses are mine.”

The rest — fees, RBF, PSBTv2, import bundles, evidence — is on the pages we’ll open. No duplication here.

What I learned

Smallest policy that fits the money.

1 key for spending, 2 when a stolen phone shouldn’t be enough, 3 when losing one shouldn’t lock you out. Annoying wallets get emptied into worse ones.

Two makers beats two phones.

MuSig2 n-of-n hides that a second device exists. 2-of-3 shows it — and that visibility is what makes coercion fail.

The phone verifies filters locally.

No address ever sent to a server. Bounded mempool window while a screen is open; confirmation still comes from a block.

Then Q&A — paper, evidence, and source are one tap away.
